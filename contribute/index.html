    How to read this page

    The map pairs 36 state/UT crop examples and technology prompts with official Rabi sowing notes where the Ministry bulletin names a state, plus a CWC reservoir-region comparison where that state is covered. A missing state note means only that the cited bulletin did not list one; it does not mean there was no sowing or water concern.

    Research limit: the map’s crop examples are not yet reconciled state-by-state against a named official production year, so they are not rankings or measured local crop profiles. The page has no live crop statistics, market prices, weather alerts, scheme eligibility, or farmer-survey results. Validate product ideas with local farmers and extension experts.

    • Weather: show the locality, issue time, and validity of an official advisory; do not present a state-level prompt as a forecast.
    • Statistics and markets: keep crop year, market, commodity, units, and reporting date attached to every figure; early sowing area is not production.
    • Schemes: link to official eligibility and application guidance; never imply that a user qualifies based on a map selection.
    • Crop-health AI: expose uncertainty and source provenance, and provide a path to local expert review.
