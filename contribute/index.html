    06 / Further reading

    Start with sources you can verify.

    These official Indian agriculture, weather, water, scheme, and market portals are references for checking assumptions. This page does not display live government data.

    How to read this page

    The map pairs illustrative crop examples with regional technology questions. Crop examples have not been reconciled state-by-state against a named official production year, so they are not current rankings or measured local profiles.

    • Weather: keep location, issue time, and validity with every official advisory.
    • Statistics and markets: attach crop year, market, commodity, units, and reporting date to each figure.
    • Schemes: link to official eligibility guidance; never infer qualification from a map selection.
    • Crop-health AI: expose uncertainty and provenance, with a path to local expert review.

    State and union-territory boundary geometry is redistributed under its upstream MIT license. See map attribution and source vintage. Boundaries are not authoritative legal determinations; validate product ideas with local farmers and agriculture experts.
